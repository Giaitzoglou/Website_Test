Brown Butter Mayo

Version 1 - Classic · Thermomix recipe · Makes about 1 kg

Ingredients

  • 160 g unsalted butter
  • 800 g mayonnaise
  • 20 g Dijon mustard
  • 20 g fresh lemon juice
  • 8 g Worcestershire sauce
  • 5 g fine sea salt
  • 2 g freshly ground black pepper

Method

  1. Place the butter into the Thermomix mixing bowl.
  2. Cook 15 min / 120°C / speed 1 with the measuring cup removed and the simmering basket on top. If needed, cook 2-4 min more until deep golden brown and nutty.
  3. Pour the browned butter, including all browned milk solids, into a jug and cool to about 40-45°C.
  4. Clean and dry the bowl. Add mayonnaise, Dijon, lemon juice, Worcestershire, salt and pepper. Mix 20 sec / speed 4.
  5. Mix 1 min / speed 4 while slowly pouring in the warm browned butter through the lid opening.
  6. Mix 20 sec / speed 4.
  7. Chill for at least 1 hour before serving.
