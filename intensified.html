Brown Butter Mayo

Version 2 - Intensified · Thermomix recipe · Makes about 1 kg

Ingredients

  • 120 g unsalted butter
  • 12 g skimmed milk powder
  • 800 g good-quality mayonnaise
  • 15 g Dijon mustard
  • 18 g fresh lemon juice
  • 8 g white wine vinegar
  • 2 g fine sea salt
  • 1 g freshly ground black pepper

Method

  1. Place the butter and skimmed milk powder into the Thermomix mixing bowl.
  2. Cook 12 min / 120°C / speed 1 with the measuring cup removed. If needed, cook 1-3 min more until the milk solids are deep golden and nutty, not black.
  3. Pour everything into a jug, including all browned specks, and cool to about 35-40°C.
  4. Clean and dry the bowl. Add mayonnaise, Dijon, lemon juice, white wine vinegar, salt and pepper. Mix 20 sec / speed 4.
  5. Mix 1 min / speed 4 while slowly pouring in the warm brown butter through the lid opening.
  6. Mix 20 sec / speed 4.
  7. Chill for at least 2 hours, ideally overnight.
